## Description

Turn any topic into a scroll-stopping faceless video in minutes — no camera, no editing skills.

Type a topic like “Why do octopuses have three hearts?”, paste an article, or write your own lines. AutoShorts writes a short script with a strong hook and a call to action, records a natural AI voiceover, adds moving backgrounds and royalty-free music, and burns in animated word-by-word captions. You get a vertical 9:16 MP4 ready for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.

Made on your PC. The AI voices run on your own computer, so your scripts stay private, voiceovers are unlimited, and everything keeps working offline after a one-time download.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Type a topic, paste an article, or write your script.
2. Pick a length (15 to 90 seconds), a format, a voice, a music mood and a look.
3. Press Generate video. Tweak any line, caption style, background or sound in the editor.
4. Export MP4 and post it with the ready-made caption and hashtags.

WHAT’S INSIDE
• Automatic scripts: facts from Wikipedia for any topic, or the best sentences of your article, turned into a hook, the key points and a call to action. Six formats: Did you know, Top list, Explainer, Story, Mystery and Motivation.
• 28 natural AI voices (American and British English) generated on your PC — or record your own voice line by line, in any language.
• 9 animated caption styles, including word-by-word pop, highlight box, neon glow, comic and word slam, with key words and numbers highlighted.
• 10 looks with animated backgrounds, emoji stickers, big number cards, a title banner and a progress bar.
• Your own photos and clips with smooth zoom-and-pan motion, or free stock footage from Pexels.
• 8 moods of original background music created by the app — royalty-free, automatically lowered under the voice, with scene cuts that land on the beat.
• Live 9:16 preview with TikTok and Shorts safe-zone guides.
• Creator Hub: every video saved on your PC, plus a post kit with captions, titles, hashtags and credits for TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

FREE AND PRO
Free: 3 exports a week in 720p with a small watermark, 3 voices, 4 looks, 4 caption styles and 3 music moods.
AutoShorts Pro: unlimited exports in 1080p Full HD with no watermark, all 28 voices, all looks, caption styles and music moods.

Voice pack: the AI voices (about 107 MB) download once on first use, then work offline.
